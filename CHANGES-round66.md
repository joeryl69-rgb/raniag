# Round 66 — The week’s picture, filed in one step

- Reports opens on this week’s picture: how many reports came in, how many are still open, how fast the first team was sent, and who is in a shelter now, including guests from outside Pamplona. This month and this year use the same dates as the dashboards. The bars show the incident types, the barangays, and the open workload.
- Filing a report is that picture. Choose the week, the month, or the year, then File this picture. There is no chapter list, no per-chapter settings, and no second form. Cases spreadsheet downloads the rows for the same dates.
- A filed copy opens as the same cards: the charts and the short tables, then Download. A draft that was already started can be filed or deleted from that page. Copy to the next period is available once a copy is filed.
- The seal line that boxed the logos is gone on every report that uses the office letterhead: the incident register, the decision report, the operations brief, the official incident file, and the filed operations record. The navy line under the office name stays. The monthly summary and the QR posters do not use that letterhead table.

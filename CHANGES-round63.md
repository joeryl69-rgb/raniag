# Round 63 — Staff dark mode, poster print, and per-account notifications

- Summary cards on the staff portal now follow dark mode. Notifications (“Matching this list”), Incidents (“Every recorded case”, “Still open”, “In progress”, “Resolved or closed”), QR Posters, accounts, and the same tiles on agency and personnel use the account’s dark card color with light numbers and captions. They were staying white, with light type that disappeared on that white.
- Helper text on dark forms is light. That includes the incident-type notes (icon search, marker color, default priority, public “what to do now”), the new-poster lines (“Staff label only…” and “Barangays that already have a poster are hidden”), announcement help, and the account-form notes. Bootstrap’s muted gray was left on the dark surface.
- Print active posters prints only the QR poster, one barangay per page, with the barangay name and the placement note when one was added. The Posters, Active, and Barangays still open tiles no longer go to the printer.
- Color themes stay available while dark mode is on. Ocean Blue, Emerald Green, Sunset Amber, Crimson Red, Royal Purple, and Slate Gray each keep their own sidebar, buttons, and dark cards. The choice is saved on that account only, so admin, agency, and personnel do not share a theme.
- Push notifications follow the signed-in account. Turning them on as admin no longer leaves them on after an agency or personnel login on the same browser. Turning them off removes that account only, and the browser subscription stays if another account on this device still wants alerts.
- The staff Support Center in dark mode uses the same dark cards, category tiles, timeline, and tips as the public help desk. The category board was still the light public form.
